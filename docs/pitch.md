# Forkcast pitch

## One sentence

Forkcast is survival of the fittest for ads: upload one product photo, get 50 Meta ads in minutes,
and let AI shoppers and then real people kill the losers until only the winner is left.

## 30 seconds

Brands spend months ideating, making, revising and signing off one campaign, and every campaign is
one big bet. AI made making ads free; knowing which ad works, and for whom, is still slow and expensive.
Forkcast takes one product photo, reads the pack so every claim is true, and generates 48 Meta ads.
A 100-person AI shopper panel screens them, the fittest survive and breed new variants, and real
people make the final call. Today the AI predicted health claims would win; this room picked
[RESULT]. That gap is exactly why we test.

## 90 seconds

- **Problem (15 s).** A food brand's campaign takes months: ideate, create, revise, get sign-off. It is
  one bet, judged after the money is spent. Tools now generate ads in seconds, so the bottleneck has
  moved from making ads to knowing which one works for whom.
- **What we built (35 s).**
  - Upload one image: we used EPIC Snax Giant Toastin' Marshmallows, off The Shelf this morning.
  - Forkcast reads the pack with vision, so copy can only claim what's printed.
  - It renders 8 scenes and writes copy for 6 behavioural levers (social proof, scarcity, health
    halo, indulgence, provenance, value): 48 ads.
  - An AI panel of shoppers screens them; the fittest 6 survive and breed copy and scene mutations
    for the next generation.
  - Every AI verdict is hashed and pushed to GitHub before people vote, so the AI is graded, not trusted.
- **Evidence (25 s).**
  - At lunch, [N] of you voted on your phones.
  - The AI bet on [AI PICK]; the room picked [HUMAN PICK]. It was off by [X] points, inside or outside
    the interval, and here's where it can and can't be trusted by audience.
  - Then the survivors' children were tested again: [RESULT].
- **Next (15 s).** Plug the same loop into Meta's Marketing API, so real CTR is the fitness, and into
  Shopify, so product photos evolve on add-to-cart. Weeks of campaign work become an afternoon,
  with evidence a brand can sign off.

## Judging fit (honest)

| Criterion | Weight | Where Forkcast scores | Risk | What raises it today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Originality & Thinking | 30% | The evolutionary loop, behavioural levers as "genes", and a sealed AI forecast graded by real people: not just another ad generator | Looks like AdCreative.ai or gimmegimme if pitched as "50 ads" | Lead with the AI-vs-room gap and the sealed seal; say "graded, not trusted" |
| Build & Execution | 30% | A real pipeline: vision pack facts → truth-guarded copy → gpt-image-2 scenes → 100-persona panel → selection → breeding → live phone voting with 90% intervals, tests at every stage, seals, security (zod, admin token, no PII) | The new campaign UI is unbuilt at 13:45 | A live demo of upload → grid evolving; show tests and the README's "beyond the hack" |
| Value & Relevance | 25% | Brands' months-long cycle → an afternoon; Really Good Culture's own business is predicting what people choose | Small convenience sample; stated intent, not CTR | Real votes today; be explicit about limits; the Meta/Shopify path |
| Demo & Communication | 15% | A visible story: one photo → 48 ads → survivors → winner → the room confirms or overturns the AI | A dashboard nobody can read in 2 minutes | Script the video around 3 moments: upload, evolution, AI vs room |

## Can it win?

**Verdict: a credible finalist, not a sure winner.**

- **Why it can win:** the 30% build score rewards depth, and few teams will have a working
  vision → generation → simulation → selection → human-validation loop. The AI-vs-room reveal is a
  live, on-brief moment for a research-led host.
- **Why it might not:**
  - "AI ad generator" is a crowded idea; judges must see the evolution and the honest grading within 10 seconds.
  - If lunch turnout is thin, the evidence is weak.
  - If the campaign screen isn't working by 16:30, the demo shrinks to scripts and a vote page.
- **To win:**
  1. Get 40+ people voting at lunch and 16:00.
  2. Ship the campaign screen.
  3. Open the video on the AI's wrong bet.
