## Problem Statement

Brand campaigns run in a slow sequence: ideate, create, revise, then wait for authorisation.
Each campaign takes months and is one big bet. AI ad tools ([gimmegimme](https://getgimmegimme.com/)
"Get 100 winning Meta ads", AdCreative.ai, Pencil) have made creation nearly free. The
bottleneck has moved. Brands now need to know which variant works for which audience before
spending, with evidence an approver will accept.

Forkcast takes one real food product from The Shelf and turns it into 6 ads, each built on one
behavioural lever. An AI persona panel forecasts the results and that forecast is sealed before
anyone votes. Real people vote at lunch. The dashboard grades the AI against the room and
produces a challenger ad, which is re-tested at 16:00.

## What Makes This Cool

- **The lunch room is the test market.** A QR code, 30 seconds, 6 ads, anonymous.
- **Sealed forecast.** The AI predictions are hashed and pushed to the public repo before the
  first vote, so git history serves as the pre-registration.
- **Levers, not lucky winners.** Each ad expresses one lever: social proof, scarcity, health
  halo, indulgence, provenance or value. Results answer "why", not just "which".
- **Loop.** Results go to Claude, which writes a challenger. A second forecast is sealed and
  the 16:00 round tests it. Judges vote live at the finals (`/vote?seg=judge`).
- **Evidence card per ad.** Creative, human rate, interval, n, AI forecast and seal on one page.
  It is the approver's artifact and attacks the authorisation delay the user named.

## Constraints

- **Clock:** 12:45 to 17:30 BST submission (≤2-min video, public repo + README). Votes must be
  collectable by the 14:00 lunch. Finals at 18:30.
- **Team:** 2 people (backend and frontend), each driving Claude Code. Specs:
  `docs/superpowers/specs/backend.md` and `docs/superpowers/specs/frontend.md`.
- **Sample:** about 40-80 attendees, a convenience sample (the hackathon crowd).
- **No ad spend:** the outcome is stated tap intent, not CTR, ROAS or sales.
- **Brief rules:** no single-prompt wrappers. Synthetic data must state how it was created and
  validated, and its limits.
- **Stack:** the `/hackathon` template (pnpm, zod contract, Hono, Next.js), the Anthropic API,
  and a cloudflared quick tunnel. The live server runs `next build` + `next start` on the
  backend laptop.

## Premises

#1 and #2 were revised after the Codex cold read.

1. **Creation is solved; validation is scarce.** The hero metric is the sealed AI forecast
   error (winner hit, MAE, Spearman) plus a trust map. "Votes saved" is a stretch goal only,
   and only with a defined baseline.
2. **6 lever variants on 1 product photo.** Every voter sees all 6 in random order
   (within-subjects), so each variant's n equals the voter count. The pooled ranking comes
   first; a segment cell may claim a winner only at n ≥ 10.
3. **Evidence = the room.** Each round's forecast is sealed before that round's first vote.
   There is a lunch round and a 16:00 challenger round. Intervals are 90% Wilson. Every
   synthetic number is labelled.
4. **Track 1, Human Truth.** Behavioural levers plus the AI-vs-human gap.
5. **Two specs, one frozen contract**, built on the `/hackathon` template.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Pre-registered Room Test
Kept as the floor and built first. 6 lever ads, a sealed forecast and one lunch round, with an
honest dashboard.

### Approach B: Adaptive Lab
Rejected. 24-48 variants with Thompson sampling seeded by the AI prior would spread ~60 voters
too thin. An allocation bug at 14:00 couldn't be recovered, and selective exposure muddies the
AI-vs-human comparison.

### Approach C: Room → Challenger loop
Chosen: A plus an evidence-driven challenger round at 16:00.

## Recommended Approach

Approach C, built as A first. **Cut rule:** if the dashboard isn't live on real data at 15:15,
drop the challenger and polish A.

```
phone /vote ──POST /api/votes──► Next proxy ──► Hono API ──append──► data/votes.jsonl (+ server `at`)
laptop /qr, /dashboard ◄── GET /api/results (3 s poll) ── buildResults(): Wilson 90%, post-stratified AI, scorecards
scripts: generate.ts → data/variants/round-1.json
         forecast.ts → data/forecasts/round-1.json (sealedAt + sha256) → git commit + push  (= pre-registration)
POST /api/challenger (admin token) → Claude → data/variants/round-2.json → forecast round 2 → seal → activeRound = 2
cloudflared tunnel → https://<random>.trycloudflare.com/vote → QR on /qr
```

- **Contract:** 4 endpoints (`GET /variants`, `POST /votes`, `GET /results`, `POST /challenger`).
  The full zod schemas are byte-identical in both specs and type-checked under `--strict`.
- **Experiment rules:**
  - Copy may only use facts printed on the pack. A deterministic guard rejects invented numbers.
  - Each forecast is sealed before votes.
  - Exposure is balanced and order is randomised per voter.
  - Stats are honest: the n ≥ 10 rule, and MAE and Spearman only over variants with n ≥ 10.
  - No PII is stored.
- **Security:**
  - Inputs are zod-validated at the API boundary.
  - Votes are idempotent on `(voterId, variantId)`.
  - The challenger endpoint needs an admin token, because attendees share the public URL.
  - Accepted risk, documented: anonymous IDs mean someone could stuff the ballot.
- **AI models:** `claude-opus-5-5` for copy and challengers. `claude-sonnet-5-5` for the
  40-persona panel, falling back to `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` if it's slow. Token usage is
  logged and reported, and no costs are quoted from memory.

- The round-1 seal commit time is earlier than the first vote's `at` timestamp.
- At least 40 voters take part at lunch.
